# MarsClock Privacy Policy

MarsClock does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

The app does not request the INTERNET permission and cannot send data 
anywhere. All tasks, notes, and reminders are stored locally on your 
device in a private database that only MarsClock can access.

The app requests the following permissions, each used only for its 
stated purpose:

- POST_NOTIFICATIONS — to deliver task reminders
- SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM — to fire reminders at the exact Mars time
- RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED — to reschedule reminders after a reboot
- WAKE_LOCK — to ensure reminders fire on time

No analytics. No tracking. No third-party SDKs. No accounts.

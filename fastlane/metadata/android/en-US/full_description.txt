A Martian timekeeper for Android.

Features:

* Live Mars Coordinated Time (MTC)
* Darian calendar with 24 months
* Add tasks and notes to any sol
* Recurring tasks (every N sols)
* Reminders at a chosen MTC time
* Live mission sols for Curiosity and Perseverance
* Jump to any Mars year
* All data stored locally, no cloud, no account

This app does NOT request the INTERNET permission.
